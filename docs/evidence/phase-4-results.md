Four language/theme combinations checked mobile 390×844 navigation, skip-link focus, htmx refresh focus and dashboard/connection/gallery/create-form scans. The S3 scenario checked invalid bucket field associations, CSRF rejection, real bucket/marker/upload navigation, Unicode original filename, byte-identical SHA-256 download, MIME/attachment headers and fixture cleanup. No CSP console violation or page error was reported by the S3 scenario. Axe found no violations for the selected rules on the scanned pages; incomplete/manual findings are retained in local reports, not interpreted as conformance.

Em 2026-10-14, Windows/amd64 com containers Linux: seis testes Chromium/axe aprovados em 41,4 s, um worker, sem retry. Quatro combinações idioma/tema verificaram viewport 390×844, foco do skip/refresh e páginas base. Zero violações axe nas regras/páginas selecionadas não equivale a conformidade WCAG; resultados incompletos/manuais ficam nos relatórios locais.

## Measurement sample / Amostra de medições

Five sequential warm dashboard navigations in the same local Chromium run (not a load test): response timing 42.3, 48.2, 49.9, 34.1 and 33.3 ms; DOMContentLoaded 86.4, 83.1, 78.7, 65.6 and 65.2 ms. A subsequent single `docker stats --no-stream` observation showed GUI 177.1 MiB and MiniStack 63.69 MiB. This is container memory after tests, not peak heap, startup time, concurrency capacity or a hardware-independent budget. Raw navigation/axe attachments and JSON report are in ignored `work/` and regenerated by [the guide](../development/phase-4.md).

Cinco navegações sequenciais aquecidas: resposta entre 33,3 e 49,9 ms; DOMContentLoaded entre 65,2 e 86,4 ms. Uma observação posterior de memória mostrou GUI 177,1 MiB e MiniStack 63,69 MiB. Não mede pico de heap, startup, capacidade concorrente ou orçamento independente do hardware. Relatórios locais em `work/` são regeneráveis pelo guia.
